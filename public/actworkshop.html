ACT for Burnout

A one-day workshop for practitioners seeing burnout in the room and wanting a clearer way to work with it. Acceptance and commitment therapy applied to clients who cannot change their circumstances quickly.

  • How burnout presents differently from depression, and what changes in your formulation
  • Working with values when a client feels they have no room to choose
  • Committed action sized for someone with no capacity left
  • Defusion for the thought patterns that keep people in overextension
  • 16 CPD points, and the ACT in Practice on-demand course included
  • Recording access afterwards, and a full materials pack

Already registered for The Burnout Reset? If you chose the complete pathway, the 12 October day is included and there is nothing further to pay. If you chose the two-day intensive + integration pathway, ACT is separate and you can book it below. Compare the pathways.
