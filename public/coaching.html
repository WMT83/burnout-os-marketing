Executive coaching

Executive Coaching with Werner Teichert.

For senior professionals working on performance, resilience, and sustainable leadership. Twelve sessions over six months. Fortnightly cadence. By application.

Apply for coaching →
Capped at six concurrent clients.

Some problems don't respond to group work.

They need a direct conversation with someone who has the clinical depth to see the pattern — and the coaching experience to help you shift it.

This is the programme for leaders, clinicians, founders, and senior operators who are clear their situation calls for one-to-one attention — either as a continuation after completing the Intensive, or as the right place to begin.

Twelve sessions across six months. Fortnightly cadence. Structured enough that each session builds on the last. Long enough to see real change.

Who this is for

If this describes your situation, your application is likely to be a match.

  • Graduates of the Burnout Recovery Intensive who want continued individual work
  • Senior professionals with specific leadership or performance challenges
  • People who value structured coaching over open-ended therapy
  • Professionals ready to commit to a six-month relationship, not a single-session fix
Who this is not for

If this describes your situation, coaching isn't the right frame.

  • People in acute clinical crisis
  • People seeking short-term support or a single-session fix
  • People seeking Medicare-rebatable therapy

If coaching isn't right for you, Werner will tell you directly when reviewing your application — and suggest what would be.

The engagement

A designed engagement, not a session count.

Twelve sessions across six months gives the work room to land. The structure has two layers: four phases that shape the trajectory, and three pattern assessments that anchor it to measurable change. Cadence is fortnightly. What happens inside each session flexes to what you're navigating.

Before you begin

Application & enrolment

A short structured application — what you're looking for, what you understand the engagement to be, and any context Werner should know. Werner reviews every application personally and responds within two business days. If coaching is the right frame for what you're navigating, you'll receive offer details and a link to book your first session. If it isn't, Werner will say so directly and point you toward what would help more. Enrolment begins with a baseline pattern assessment using validated instruments from the clinical literature.

Phase one

Orient

Pattern walkthrough using your baseline assessment. Three priority areas agreed. Early work begins.

Phase two

Foundations

Pattern-level work on each priority. Tools and frames introduced as needed, not on a schedule.

Phase three

Application

Priorities tested under live pressure. Real decisions, real demands, real integration work. Mid-point pattern re-assessment provides an honest read on what's shifting and what isn't.

Phase four

Integration

Final pattern assessment. Articulation of your signature pattern — the personal map you take forward. Honest close-out: new package, ad-hoc continuation, graduation, or alternative pathway, whichever is right.

Three fixed anchors

Baseline, mid-point, final

Three pattern assessments using validated instruments from the clinical literature. They're the structure you can measure against. Everything else is the work.

The engagement includes

Engagement requires an ongoing relationship with a general practitioner, with a consultation in the past six months.
