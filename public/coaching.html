Space for the work
that needs your attention.

For senior leaders, clinicians, founders and operators who want individual attention on a specific leadership or performance challenge. A structured six-month engagement gives you time to test changes under real pressure.

A designed
engagement.

Four phases, supported by initial, midpoint and final pattern assessments.

01

Orient

Explore your starting patterns and agree on your priority areas.

02

Foundations

Work on each priority, introducing tools and frames as needed.

03

Application

Test the work through live decisions and demands. Review what is changing at the midpoint.

04

Integration

Consolidate what you have learned and agree the next step.

The support
between sessions.

The engagement includes pattern assessments, between-session email access, a monthly reflection journal reviewed by Werner, and BurnoutOS platform access. Investment and payment options are shared after application review.

Before you apply.

This is a six-month coaching engagement, not therapy or crisis care. Sessions are not offered as Medicare-rebatable psychological treatment. Participation requires an ongoing relationship with a GP and a consultation within the past six months.
