If you've been operating like this for a while

You're still delivering. The team still relies on you. To everyone watching, you're coping, possibly better than most.

But something has shifted.

Sleep is harder than it was. Recovery from a hard week takes longer than it used to. Slowing down feels strangely dangerous. Like the moment you stop is the moment everything catches up with you. You've started to wonder whether what you're doing is sustainable, and whether you'd even know how to do anything else.

Most of the advice out there doesn't quite land. Take a break. Practise self-care. Set boundaries. It's not wrong. It's just not enough, and at some level you already know that.

This masterclass is for the senior leader or clinician who's reached that point.

What you'll leave with

Three session outcomes, then the three-phase pathway they point toward. 90 minutes covers all of it.

01  / Reset

Stabilise your capacity.

You'll leave knowing exactly where your burnout sits and what it would take to rebuild a foundation that holds under pressure.

Phase 1
02  / Redesign

Redesign what's driving the overload.

Phase 2
03  / Sustain

Build a rhythm that doesn't drift.

You'll see what ongoing clinical support and accountability looks like so the gains from reset and redesign compound rather than erode.

Phase 3

The session is complete in itself. No obligation to proceed. You leave with a clear read on where you sit and what your next step looks like.

Built to tell you about you

Burnout advice is written for the average person, which is why it keeps not landing. A baseline starts from where you are, so the way forward stops being one more thing to try and becomes a decision you can make with clear eyes.

Run by Werner Teichert, Clinical Psychologist (AHPRA). The masterclass is education and structured self-assessment. It is not therapy, not individual clinical assessment, and not a substitute for crisis care. If you are in acute psychological distress, the appropriate path is direct contact with a crisis service or your GP rather than a 90-minute group session.

About Werner Teichert

Werner is a Clinical Psychologist registered with AHPRA. He works clinically with senior leaders and clinicians navigating chronic stress and burnout, and is the founder of BurnoutOS, a clinical recovery pathway built specifically for high-functioning professionals whose burnout has gone beyond what general advice can address.
