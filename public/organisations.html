When people are struggling, a general wellbeing initiative may miss the conditions driving the problem.

Discuss a focused consulting engagement to understand overload and identify practical priorities for your organisation.

Discuss your organisation

A defined engagement

Agree on the question, the people involved, the evidence needed and the decisions the work should support.

Start with the question you need answered.

Where is pressure building? What is making it harder for teams to do their work? Which changes are within leadership’s control?

The scope follows your situation. Assessment, interviews, facilitated discussion and reporting can be considered as part of the agreed work.

From an unclear concern
to practical priorities.

01

Clarify

Scope and context

Define the problem before choosing the intervention.

Agree on the purpose, participants, methods and information boundaries. Clarify what the engagement will deliver.

02

Understand

People and working conditions

Bring the relevant evidence together.

Explore demands, support, working relationships and organisational patterns. Interpret findings in their context.

03

Act

Leadership decisions and next steps

Decide what to change first.

Discuss the findings, prioritise feasible actions and agree on ownership and how progress will be reviewed.

Clear scope. Useful outputs.

A proposal sets out the agreed methods, deliverables, timing and fees. Depending on the question, outputs may include a findings summary, leadership debrief and prioritised action plan.

This is organisational consulting. It does not provide individual diagnosis, clinical treatment or a certification of legal compliance.
