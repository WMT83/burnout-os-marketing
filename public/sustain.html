Why a membership

The hardest part of recovery is not getting there. It's staying there.

Capacity rebuilt in Phase 1 and a system redesigned in Phase 2 will hold for a while on their own. But pressure returns, old patterns resurface, and without a cadence to catch the slide, most people are back where they started within a year. The Sustain Lab exists for exactly this. It is not more content. It is the ongoing rhythm — a monthly point of contact, a quarterly recalibration, and a community that keeps you honest — that turns a good eight weeks into a durable way of working.

What is included

Everything you need to hold the line.

Monthly live sessions

Q&A, hot seats, and scenario work with Werner. Bring what is live for you and work it in real time with the group.

Quarterly reset

A structured recalibration every three months — what is working, what has slipped, and what to adjust next.

Monthly tool drops

A new template or tool each month — quarterly reset frameworks, decision hygiene checklists, travel and peak-period rules.

Early Warning Dashboard

Track your leading indicators so you see the slide coming before it costs you, not after.

If-Then plans for peak periods

Pre-built protocols for the predictable high-load windows — audits, launches, board cycles — so they do not undo your progress.

Peer community and pods

Ongoing access to a community of people who get it, with optional accountability pods for closer support.

Eligibility

For people who have done the work.

The Sustain Lab is open to anyone who has completed Phase 1 (Performance Reset) or Phase 2 (Operating System Redesign). The container works because everyone in it shares the same foundation and language. If you have not done either phase yet, the right place to start is the masterclass or Phase 1 — then the Sustain Lab is here when you are ready to maintain what you have built.
