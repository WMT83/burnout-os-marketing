For clinicians · the presentation

Nothing has shifted.

You know this client. Coming for months. Low mood, broken sleep, irritable, slower than they used to be. You formulated depression, you treated it well, and they are no better.

Burnout presents like a lot of other things. Treated as depression it does not shift, because the driver is still there every Monday morning.

This programme trains the sequence that targets the driver. How to assess, formulate and treat the presentations that keep circling back.

32 CPD points. 30 contact hours. In collaboration with PsySSA.

What you take back to practice

The sequence · targeting client capacity in phases

Reset. Redesign. Sustain.

Run the sessions out of order and it stalls. That is the most common reason improvised burnout treatment fails.

Reset · Sessions 1 to 4

Restore capacity first

Baseline and overload, the recovery cycle, sleep anchors, and micro-recovery. Capacity has to come back before anything else holds.

Redesign · Sessions 5 to 6

Change what keeps it switched on

Rumination and the switched-on pattern. Perfectionism, values, and the shutdown ritual that keeps the tank empty.

Sustain · Sessions 7 to 8

Make the change hold

Boundary scripts and capacity signalling, then the Personal Recovery Protocol the client carries forward.
